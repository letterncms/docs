In Lettern you design in the browser, on the real site. There's no code to write and nothing to build: what you see in the editor is what visitors get.

Design works in layers. The **design system** is your theme: a few decisions about colors, fonts and spacing that every page follows, with contrast checked for you. **Templates** frame your pages with a header, a footer and a layout. **Components** are pieces you reuse, like a card or a call to action. And every block can be styled, animated and made interactive on its own.

Every style, on every layer, is a name from the design system rather than a typed-in value, so what you, the assistant and plugins add always looks like your site. Developers and AI agents change the very same design, through the API, MCP or the files in git. See [Project structure](/reference/project-structure).
